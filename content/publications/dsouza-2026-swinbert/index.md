abstract: Video captioning lets computers understand visual content and describe it
  in natural language. Traditional CNN-RNN architectures struggle with long-range
  temporal dependencies and contextual relationships in video. This work uses a Swin
  Transformer to extract video features and a BERT decoder to generate captions, combining
  semantic features to produce fluent, context-appropriate descriptions. The system
  shows reasonable captioning performance on the MSVD and MSR-VTT benchmarks while
  remaining efficient enough for near real-time use, with applications in video summarization
  and assistive technologies.
