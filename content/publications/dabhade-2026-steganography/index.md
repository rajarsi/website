abstract: Image steganography preserves privacy by embedding hidden information within
  a cover image. This research presents a streamlined Convolutional Neural Network
  (CNN) model that embeds a concealed image inside a cover image and retrieves it
  using an encoder-decoder design. Unlike conventional least-significant-bit techniques,
  it spreads and compresses the hidden image's information across all available bits.
  Evaluated with SSIM and PSNR, the method reports PSNR 44.37 and SSIM 0.9956, outperforming
  existing deep-learning steganographic methods with better imperceptibility, security,
  robustness and hiding capacity.
